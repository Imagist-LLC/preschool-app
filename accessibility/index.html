Accessibility at Preschool.app

At Preschool.app, we believe every child deserves a joyful, inclusive learning experience — no matter their abilities, background, or language. Accessibility is not an add-on — it’s built into our foundation.

🧒 Designed for Toddlers

  • Large, tappable targets for little hands
  • No reading required to navigate
  • Audio and visual feedback on every interaction
  • Drag and tap options for all interactive elements
  • No ads, no distractions — just pure learning

🧠 Built for Every Brain

  • Supports cognitive diversity with Montessori-style modules
  • Modular pathing allows free exploration or structured progression
  • No timers, scoreboards, or punishments — just encouragement
  • Semantic animations respect sensory sensitivity

🌍 Language-Ready

  • 50+ supported languages with localized examples
  • Every letter, word, and symbol can be spoken aloud
  • High-quality, novelty, and personal voice options
  • Emoji vocabulary reinforces meaning visually

🎧 Audio & Speech

  • Speech synthesis tuned for toddler comprehension
  • Supports VoiceOver and system spoken content
  • Customizable voice selection

📱 Platform-Friendly

  • Available on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro
  • Integrates with Widgets, Live Activities, and Shortcuts
  • Supports system accessibility settings like Reduce Motion
  • Built using native Apple accessibility frameworks

👨‍👩‍👧 Made for Families

  • Quiet progress tracking — no accounts needed
  • Parent-friendly, child-first design
  • No dark patterns or pay-to-play traps
  • Works offline and in shared environments
