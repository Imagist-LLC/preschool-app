The Science Behind Our Preschool Learning App

Parents often worry about screen time, especially for young children. But not all screen time is the same. Research shows that well-designed interactive educational apps can enhance early learning, improving memory, literacy, and math skills when used appropriately.[1]

“Studies have found that when preschoolers actively tap, swipe, and respond to content, they form stronger memory traces and understanding.” [2]

Unlike passive screen time (like TV or non-educational videos), interactive learning apps actively engage a child’s brain, reinforcing essential concepts through touch, sound, and problem-solving.[3]

1. Research Shows the Right Kind of Digital Learning Works

A 2021 meta-analysis of 36 studies found that digital learning apps had an overall positive effect on literacy and math skills—with an average improvement of +0.30 standard deviations.[4]

“Notably, effects were strongest for preschoolers, suggesting that ages 3–5 reap substantial gains from interactive app experiences.” [4]
