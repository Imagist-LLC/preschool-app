The Science Behind Our Preschool Learning App

Parents often worry about screen time, especially for young children. However, not all screen time is the same. Research indicates that well-designed interactive educational apps can enhance early learning, improving memory, literacy, and math skills when used appropriately.[1]

“Studies have found that when preschoolers actively tap, swipe, and respond to content, they form stronger memory traces and understanding.” [2]

Unlike passive screen time (like TV or non-educational videos), interactive learning apps actively engage a child’s brain, reinforcing essential concepts through touch, sound, and problem-solving.[3]

1. Research Shows the Right Kind of Digital Learning Works

A 2021 meta-analysis of 36 studies found that digital learning apps had an overall positive effect on literacy and math skills—with an average improvement of +0.30 standard deviations.[4]

“Notably, effects were strongest for preschoolers, suggesting that ages 3–5 reap substantial gains from interactive app experiences.” [4]

Another comprehensive review involving approximately 5,000 children under age 6 using educational apps found the most significant improvements in early math, followed by language and literacy skills.[5]

2. Active Engagement Enhances Learning

Studies have found that when preschoolers actively tap, swipe, and respond to content, they form stronger memory traces and understanding.[2]

However, experts recommend using educational apps to supplement traditional preschool activities, not to replace them entirely.[6]

Additionally, studies warn that too much interactivity or irrelevant multimedia can backfire, overstimulating children and reducing comprehension.[7]

3. Quality Matters: Not All Apps Are Created Equal

The 2021 meta-analysis noted that its positive results might not generalize to the tens of thousands of poorly designed apps on the market.[4]

Apps teaching basic number sense, letter recognition, phonological awareness, and vocabulary showed clear learning advantages over traditional practice. These findings underscore that app-based education, when done right, can match or even surpass some traditional methods in teaching effectiveness.[8]

4. Our Commitment to Research-Based Design

Our preschool learning app is grounded in this body of research. We focus on:

  • Active Engagement: Designing activities that require children to interact meaningfully with content.
  • Educational Objectives: Emphasizing foundational skills like math, literacy, and critical thinking.
  • Balanced Design: Avoiding overstimulation by ensuring multimedia elements serve clear educational purposes.
  • Supplementary Use: Encouraging parents and educators to integrate our app into a broader educational framework.

By aligning our app with established educational principles, we aim to provide a valuable tool that supports and enhances traditional preschool education.
